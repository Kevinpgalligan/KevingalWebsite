I've uploaded a digitised version of my recipe notebook to the site. To be clear, they're not *my* recipes, they're just recipes I've jotted down from cooking websites or that friends have shared with me. All of them are vegan-friendly.

Check it out [HERE](/recipes.html)!

Hopefully it's okay that I'm sharing other people's recipes as long as I give them credit?? It's not like this website is a serious competitor to actual cooking websites, I'm just hosting them here (in a shortened, image-less form, sometimes with my own tweaks (always less onion 🧅🤢)), so that I can access them more easily and share them with friends. It's still a work-in-progress, and suggestions are welcome to fill any gaps in my repertoire!

Why vegan-only? I've been vegan for about 2 and a half years now. Before this post, I wrote, in an explosion of sanctimonious and obnoxious vegan rage, a draft about my experience with veganism. However, I got stalled because I felt like I wasn't authoritative or knowledgeable enough to give satisfactory answers to some of the questions that arose during the writing. I think I'll go ahead and post it, though, and just make it clear that I don't have the answers to every single ethical dilemma, all the nutritional science, etc. I'd also like to write some more programming content this year. Stay tuned, non-existent readers!
