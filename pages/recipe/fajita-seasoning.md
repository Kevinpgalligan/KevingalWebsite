name: Fajita seasoning
source: spendwithpennies.com

### Ingredients
* chili powder (1 tbsp)
* ground cumin (2 tsp)
* smoked paprika (2 tsp)
* garlic powder (2 tsp)
* granulated sugar (1 tsp) [I just use whatever type of sugar]
* black pepper (1/2 tsp)
* salt (1/2 tsp)
* cayenne pepper (1/4 tsp)

### Instructions
1. Mix everything in a bowl.
2. That's it.

For the wraps themselves, I just fry up veggies (pepper, mushroom, maybe tofu, whatever) and boil some rice. That forms the filling, along with salad stuff like lettuce & sweetcorn. Also good to add some hot sauce like Valentina.
