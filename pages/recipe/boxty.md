name: Boxty
source: thefieryvegetarian.com

"Boxty (Irish: bacstaí or steaimpí) is a traditional Irish potato pancake." ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxty))

### Ingredients
* potato (450g)
* flour (140g)
* baking powder (2 tsp)
* salt (1 tsp)
* oat milk (280ml)
* vegetable-based spread, for frying (up to 50g)

### Instructions
1. Boil 230g potato chunks in (in salted water) for 10 mins until tender, then mash.
2. Mix flour, salt & baking powder.
3. Add the mash & milk, whisk until smooth.
4. Grate 220g potato, add it, mix.
5. Let batter rest for 5 mins. Breathe.
6. Cook batter in ~60g dollops on low-medium heat, 4 mins on each side.
