name: Guacamole
source: Sonia

### Ingredients
* avocado (6)
* black beans (1 tin)
* sweetcorn (1 tin)
* lemon (a squeeze)
* salt (a bit)
* possibly other seasonings, I forget

### Instructions
1. Cut up the avocados and put in a big bowl (slice in half, take out the center, make a cross-hatch pattern in each half with your knife, then scoop out with a spoon)
2. Mash.
3. Add in the other ingredients and mix.
