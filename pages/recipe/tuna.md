name: Not Tuna
source: Sonia

This is really nice in a sandwich or as a standalone snack.

### Ingredients
* chickpeas (from tins)
* seaweed sheets
* vegan mayonnaise [I'm looking for an alternative to Hellman's and Heinz, since both of those are on various boycott lists]

### Instructions
1. Mash up the chickpeas in a big mixing bowl.
2. Break up the seaweed sheets into small pieces, mix in.
3. Add the mayo and mix thoroughly.
