name: Bean chili
source: frommybowl.com

### Ingredients:
* bell peppers (2)
* small onion (1)
* ribs of celery (3) [or bok choy]
* garlic (3 cloves)
* carrots (3, small)
* chili powder (4 tbsp)
* cumin (1 tsp)
* oregano (1 tsp)
* salt (1 tsp)
* kidney beans (2 cans)
* black beans (2 cans)
* crushed tomatoes (2 cans / 800g)
* vegetable broth (235ml) [or just water]

### Instructions
1. Warm large pot over med-high heat. Add 80ml water, onion, celery (or bok choy), and bell peppers.
2. Cook until all water evaporates. Once pot dry and veg starts turning golden-brown, add 60ml water. This should take ~10 mins total.
3. Reduce to med. Add garlic, carrots and all the spices. Leave for 2-3 mins, adding water if it sticks.
4. Add beans, tomato & broth. Bring to boil, then reduce heat & simmer (with lid) for 10 mins.
5. Remove lid, cook for 5-7 mins until carrots tender and mixture has desired thickness. Stir occasionally to avoid sticking.

### Notes
* Store in fridge up to 5 days.
* Freeze up to 1 month.
* Nice with potatoes.
